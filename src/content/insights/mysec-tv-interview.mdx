Drago Gvozdanovic appeared as a guest on MySec.TV on 28 April 2021 to talk about Cynterra's recent win: supplying the Australian Federal Government's Digital Transformation Agency (DTA) with one of Cynterra's Secure Internet Gateway solutions.

The conversation covered the thinking behind the platform: replacing consolidated big-iron gateways with tailored, per-application cloud gateways that can be configured in under a day, and what the DTA selection signals for cloud-based security in Australian government.

For the full background on the DTA contract, see [the announcement](/insights/dta-secure-internet-gateway).
