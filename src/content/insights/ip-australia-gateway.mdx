Cynterra has landed a deal with IP Australia to secure the agency's online applications, as reported by [CRN on 21 April 2021](https://www.crn.com.au/news/canberra-mssp-cynterra-scores-with-ip-australia-551489).

IP Australia administers intellectual property rights and legislation for patents, trade marks, registered designs, and plant breeder's rights, and sits within the Department of Industry, Innovation and Science.

The deal, worth **$250,000 per year**, provides cloud-based web gateways for the agency's trademark and patent systems and other online applications. It was awarded **in partnership with NTT Ltd**, who provide 24/7 monitoring through their Security Operations Center, along with threat escalation and service management.

The gateways deliver real-time reporting and data collection, including geo-spatial data and updated threat information, letting the agency see threats posed to their gateways and who is accessing data from where.

Cynterra reported a **150% speed increase** on IP Australia's online applications compared with non-cloud solutions, and said at the time it was trialling gateways with a range of government departments to protect classified communications up to OFFICIAL: Sensitive and PROTECTED.

Co-founder Paul Heaton framed the win as a milestone: validation of cloud-based solutions and of Australian cybersecurity capability, and a boost to public confidence that national interests are being kept safe by an Australian company. He said Cynterra was proud to be protecting the agency that holds the intellectual property behind much of the innovation in the nation's future.

*Source: CRN, 21 April 2021.*
