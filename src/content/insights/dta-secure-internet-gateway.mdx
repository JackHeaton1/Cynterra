Cynterra has won a contract with the Australian Government's Digital Transformation Agency (DTA) to provide a new-generation Secure Internet Gateway (SIG), as first reported by David Swan in *The Australian* on 26 April 2021.

Drago Gvozdanovic said the gateway was the result of years of research and development, and would let the DTA configure secure environments in **under a day**.

His argument was structural. Government agencies have historically relied on big-iron consolidated gateways that must continually grow in capacity to handle every varying requirement. That approach is expensive, slow, and creates more opportunities for attack.

Cynterra's secure cloud platform takes the opposite approach: an architecture that tailors security to a specific application and its requirements. Tailoring per application reduces the attack surface of the organisation. If one environment is compromised, the others continue to operate independently.

He said the platform exceeded expectations by delivering tailored per-environment gateways that were up and running almost immediately, and encouraged CIOs, CISOs, and CEOs across the country to question their current gateway solution.

At the time, Cynterra said it was looking to win further government contracts.

*Source: The Australian, 26 April 2021, by David Swan.*
