Cynterra was selected as a finalist in the **InnovationAus 2021 Awards for Excellence**, announced 10 October 2021.

Cynterra was recognised in the **Cybersecurity category** (sponsored by Mimecast) for its offerings in security compliance, data protection, visibility, and threat protection services for government and private organisations, with a product portfolio built around securing network services, cloud data, and content distribution via Australian Signals Directorate (ASD) compliant, iRAP assessed gateways within public clouds.

The category's fellow finalists were Avertro, BRYK Group, Haventec, and the Office of the eSafety Commissioner. Winners were announced on 1 December 2021 at the Overseas Passenger Terminal in Sydney, with Haventec taking the Cybersecurity category.

The finalist listing is available on [InnovationAus](https://www.innovationaus.com/innovationaus-2021-awards-cybersecurity-finalists/).
